How it works & FAQ

Can I change the store-style layout?

Yes. Enter your own store information, upload an authorized logo, add or remove items, and edit the messages, tax and payment details. The narrow preview updates as you type.

How do I print or save the receipt?

Complete the required fields and choose Print / Save as PDF. For a thermal printer, select its 80 mm paper option if available. Available paper settings depend on your browser and printer. [developer.mozilla.org](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/%40page)

Does the barcode prove a purchase?

No. It only represents the reference digits you enter. This tool does not verify transactions or process payments.
