How it works & FAQ

Can I change the details from the sample receipt?

Yes. Enter your own store identity, items, prices, salesperson, tax, payment label, returns message, logo and receipt reference.

How do I print a narrow receipt?

Use Print / Save as PDF and choose an 80 mm paper size if your printer or print dialog offers one. Browser PDF output may instead use standard page sizes.

Does the barcode connect to a store system?

No. It encodes only the digits you enter. It does not create a transaction in a payment processor, point-of-sale system or store database.
