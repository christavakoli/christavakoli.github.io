How it works & FAQ

How do I make an invoice?

Enter your business and customer details, add line items, then review the live preview. Use Print / Save as PDF when it looks right.

How are tax and discounts calculated?

The fixed discount is deducted from the line-item subtotal first. The selected tax percentage is then applied to the remaining amount.

Is my invoice saved?

No. This version does not automatically store your entries. Print or save the completed invoice before closing the tab.
