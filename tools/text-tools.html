How it works & FAQ

How is reading time estimated?

The estimate uses 200 words per minute. Your actual reading speed may differ.

What does the comparison highlight?

It compares complete lines in order, highlighting lines removed from the original and lines added to the revision.

Will cleaning change my text?

Yes. Cleanup and case conversion replace the contents of the editor. Copy your original text first if you need to keep it.
